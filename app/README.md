Vacation Planner App

Purpose
The Vacation Planner App is an Android application designed to help users plan and organize their vacations by allowing them to:

Add vacation details including destination, flights, hotel, dates, number of people, and excursions.
Manage and track various excursions for each vacation, including setting specific dates for each excursion.
Share vacation details with others via email, SMS, or clipboard.
Set alerts for vacation start and end dates to remind users of their upcoming trips.


How to Operate the Application


Application Guide
Starting from the main page, you have two options: Plan a Vacation or View Vacations.

1. Planning a Vacation:
   Selecting Plan a Vacation directs you to a new page where you can input all the necessary details for your vacation.
   After submitting your vacation information, you will be taken to a summary page displaying all the details you just entered.
   On this page, you have several options:
   Save the vacation.
   Delete the vacation.
   Share the vacation details with others.
   Edit the information.
   View Excursions to select activities that complement your vacation.
2. Managing Excursions:
   Selecting View Excursions will redirect you to a screen where you can choose multiple excursions to add to your vacation.

Upon selecting an excursion, you will be prompted to choose a date. The app ensures that the excursion dates fall within your vacation period.

Once you've selected all your excursions, pressing the Confirm button will take you to the excursion details page, where you can:

View all selected excursions.
Edit the details of each excursion.
Delete any excursions if needed.
Save the excursions to your vacation.
After saving or deleting excursions, you will be redirected back to the main screen, and the appropriate function will be executed based on your choice.

3. Viewing Vacations:
   From the main screen, pressing View Vacations allows you to see a list of all the vacations you've created, where you can manage or view details of each vacation.
